Press kit

One Million Likes

Funky-Jazz-Hip Hop · Munich

One Million Likes is an eight-piece band from Munich. Formerly known as Hamperer, the group reformed in 2020 when frontman Miguel joined — rapper, writer, and producer with an international background — alongside a new saxophonist and multi-instrumentalist José.

The lineup blends funky rap with jazzy character: decades of experience across genres, brought together on stage with horns, keys, guitar, bass, drums, and live sound.

Frontman Miguel also releases solo work as M.Conradi. With One Million Likes, the band targets nothing less than a million likes — or two.
